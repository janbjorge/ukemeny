## 1. Does a dinner fit (src/core/diet.py)

- Ingredient traits from Jev (FACTS.md §6); rules below = code, computed at runtime from dish facts, so new rule needs no new data (FACTS.md §4).
- Nothing swapped (jb 2026-10-22: "We can filter on the algergies, but i do not want the replace x with y options, ex. i think had some logic that says; replace look with vaarlook"): rule drops every dinner with ingredient it covers; first such ingredient says why («inneholder gluten (spagetti)»). Before: rule suggested swaps on card («Bytt: glutenfri pasta · vårløk for løk»), ranked fewer-swap dinners first; Stavanger 2026-10-22 «Glutenfri» kept 1 915 dinners with swaps, 931 without; «Uten løk» 1 942 and 824; «Lav FODMAP» 802 and 106.
- Onion (`avoid`): onion-family ingredient drops dinner. With `onion_ok` (vårløk; site sets it for «Uten løk»), that onion fine.
- Gluten (`avoid`): "yes" ingredient drops dinner. "maybe" (stock, sausages, spice mixes) = note to check label.
- Garlic: note by default; `avoid` drops dinner.
- Lactose: grams per portion from parts' grams per portion, counting only matprat dairy departments (`/melk/melkeprodukter`, `/ost`, or no department), at 0 / 0.1 / 3.0 / 4.7 g per 100 g for none / trace / some / high. Jev called akevitt, vineddik, riseddik lactose-rich; matprat departments set by hand, so they decide. Above limit (3 g for «lite laktose», conservative; many tolerate far more per meal), dinner dropped.
- Units to grams rough (`GRAMS_PER_UNIT`): 1 dl = 100 g, 1 ss = 15 g, 1 stk = 100 g (medium onion or carrot), 1 beger = 300 g. Grams per portion rounded to 5 g in dishes.json (src/core/dishes.py), moves few small amounts to 0.
- Kids: with `small_kids` (CLI profile), chili heat + choking risks (whole nuts, sausages, cherry tomatoes, grapes) = notes in CLI text output. Site + API show neither: too many to read, name ingredient after ingredient (jb 2026-10-13). Profile says only small kids exist, not ages.
- Exclusion rules (`Household.avoid`: vegetar, vegansk, pescetarianer, melkefri, uten svin, uten alkohol, allergies) strict: ingredient that holds item, may hold it (Level.MAYBE: buljong for selleri, pølser for melk), or never asked about drops dinner. Vegetar = no meat, fish, crustaceans, molluscs (cheese counts vegetarian, any rennet); pescetarianer = no meat; vegansk = nothing animal, also lists meat, fish, milk, egg so one wrong answer can't let dinner through.
- Lav FODMAP enables onion (vårløk fine), gluten, garlic, lactose rules, then drops dinner with high-FODMAP ingredient those rules don't judge (kikerter, sopp, epler); dairy counts by lactose. Ignores amounts, so rough.
- Lavkarbo sums carbs per portion from grams + Jev bucket per 100 g (1, 6, 25 or 70 g, bucket middle), drops dinners above 20 g, guess to tune. Dry rice + pasta weighed dry, as matprat lists.
- Gravid drops ingredients Mattilsynet advises against or to limit (røkelaks, spekeskinke, lever, tunfisk, kveite, wine). Judges ingredients, not cooking, so spekeskinke baked on pizza + wine cooked in sauce still drop dinner.

- Prep time uses matprat buckets: «rask», «kjapp», «enkel», «hverdag» = under 20 min; «under N min» = strictest bucket within N (under 30 → under 20, under 45 → under 40, under en time → up to 60); over 90 min ignored.
