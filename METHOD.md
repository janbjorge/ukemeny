# METHOD

Every simplification and judgement call behind what the site suggests. Add to it whenever you make one. Facts about the sources are in FACTS.md.

## 1. Does a dinner fit (src/core/diet.py)

- Each ingredient's traits come from Jev (FACTS.md §6); the rules below are code, worked out at runtime from a dish's facts, so a new rule needs no new data (FACTS.md §4).
- Nothing is swapped (jb 2026-10-22: "We can filter on the algergies, but i do not want the replace x with y options, ex. i think had some logic that says; replace look with vaarlook"): a rule leaves out every dinner with an ingredient it is about, and the first such ingredient says why («inneholder gluten (spagetti)»). Until then a rule suggested swaps on the card («Bytt: glutenfri pasta · vårløk for løk») and ranked dinners needing fewer swaps first; in Stavanger on 2026-10-22 «Glutenfri» kept 1 915 dinners with swaps and keeps 931 without, «Uten løk» 1 942 and 824, «Lav FODMAP» 802 and 106.
- Onion (`avoid`): an onion-family ingredient leaves the dinner out. With `onion_ok` set (vårløk; the site sets it for «Uten løk»), that onion itself is fine.
- Gluten (`avoid`): a "yes" ingredient leaves the dinner out. A "maybe" (stock, sausages, spice mixes) is a note to check the label.
- Garlic: a note by default; `avoid` leaves the dinner out.
- Lactose: grams per portion from the parts' grams per portion, counting only matprat's dairy departments (`/melk/melkeprodukter`, `/ost`, or no department), at 0 / 0.1 / 3.0 / 4.7 g per 100 g for none / trace / some / high. Jev called akevitt, vineddik and riseddik lactose-rich; matprat's departments are set by hand, so they decide. Above the limit (3 g for «lite laktose», a conservative choice; many tolerate far more in one meal), the dinner is left out.
- Units to grams are rough (`GRAMS_PER_UNIT`): 1 dl = 100 g, 1 ss = 15 g, 1 stk = 100 g (a medium onion or carrot), 1 beger = 300 g. Grams per portion are rounded to 5 g in dishes.json (src/core/dishes.py), which moves a few small amounts to 0.
- Kids: with `small_kids` (the CLI's profile), chili heat and choking risks (whole nuts, sausages, cherry tomatoes, grapes) are notes in the CLI's text output. Neither the site nor the API shows them: they were too many to read, and they name ingredient after ingredient (jb 2026-10-13). The profile says only that there are small kids, not their ages.
- Rules that leave something out (`Household.avoid`: vegetar, vegansk, pescetarianer, melkefri, uten svin, uten alkohol and the allergies) are strict: an ingredient that holds the item, may hold it (Level.MAYBE: buljong for selleri, pølser for melk), or was never asked about leaves the dinner out. Vegetar is no meat, fish, crustaceans or molluscs (cheese counts as vegetarian, whatever the rennet); pescetarianer is no meat; vegansk is nothing of animal origin, and also lists meat, fish, milk and egg so one wrong answer does not let a dinner through.
- Lav FODMAP turns on the onion (vårløk fine), gluten, garlic and lactose rules, and then leaves out a dinner with a high-FODMAP ingredient those rules do not judge (kikerter, sopp, epler); dairy counts by its lactose. It ignores amounts, so it is rough.
- Lavkarbo sums carbohydrate per portion from the grams and Jev's bucket per 100 g (1, 6, 25 or 70 g, the middle of each bucket), and leaves out dinners above 20 g, a guess to tune. Dry rice and pasta are weighed dry, as matprat lists them.
- Gravid leaves out ingredients Mattilsynet advises against or to limit (røkelaks, spekeskinke, lever, tunfisk, kveite, wine). It judges ingredients, not cooking, so spekeskinke baked on a pizza and wine cooked in a sauce still leave a dinner out.

## 2. Asking Jev

- Jev is pinned to `jev-1.14.0` (src/pipeline/datasets.py `JEV_MODEL`; jb 2026-10-22: "lets pin to jev-1.14.0"). `jev-latest` went from 1.13.0 to 1.14.0 under a build on 2026-10-22, and that build changed a label of 596 of 1 741 names, 360 of them matprat's, asked with no dinner: the model change alone does that. A pin does not make a build repeat itself, though: Jev answers the same request with other probabilities (erter, below).
- traits.json is a vocabulary we keep, not a build's output (jb 2026-10-22: "We need be independent of what they do", "model our internals according to OUR needs"; docs/plan-model.md): a row per ingredient name with its facts, our `category`, its `product` and who answered. A wrong row is fixed by hand and marked `hand`, since asking Jev again only re-rolls it. It was seeded once, on 2026-10-22, from the build of 2026-10-14 (jev-1.13.0) by a throwaway script: the category from the matprat line for the 1 148 names matprat uses; for 54 of the 596 names only godt.no uses, from the matprat name with the same words once the offer filter's fillers are dropped (core.matching `_FILLER`: «erter» takes «frosne erter»'s, «urter» «friske urter»'s), which moved timian, rosmarin, chili and urter to /grønnsaker, where matprat files the fresh herb, and urter out of /fisk og sjømat; for the other 542, Jev's pick of 2026-10-14. «tørket chili» followed «rød chili» into /grønnsaker that way and was put back in /krydder by hand: a dried spice is its own product. Then 61 rows by hand: FACTS.md §6's known wrong answers, every row tests/test_traits.py flagged (a /kjøtt row without meat, a plant row with egg, pork «yes» on a sauce, a category Jev picked from the wrong dinner: gulost as /egg, kveite as /pasta/ris, lange as /grønnsaker), and a few read beside them. Two rows are right against a rule and named as exceptions: hvalkjøtt (meat, sold with the fish) and ferdig potetmos (milk). dishes.json's parts took their row's category: 347 parts over 20 names moved.
- A label is "yes" when its probability is at least 0.5, and "maybe" when yes and maybe together are (src/pipeline/jev.py `_label`). For lactose, some+high ≥ 0.5 picks between some and high, otherwise between none and trace.
- The kids question says "toddlers", not the household's ages: nothing about the household is sent.
- A yes/maybe/no label (each allergen and Item) is "yes" from 0.5, and "maybe" when yes and maybe reach 0.5 together, as gluten. FODMAP is "high" from 0.5 and "moderate" when high and moderate reach 0.5; pregnancy "avoid" and "limit" the same way; carbohydrate is the likeliest bucket.
- An offer counts as an ingredient from probability 0.5 (src/app/build.py `MATCH_THRESHOLD`).
- A new name, one the vocabulary has no row for, is drafted once (src/app/build.py `draft`): its category from the matprat line when there is one; else from a name in the vocabulary with the same words once the offer filter's fillers are dropped (core.matching `words`; «tørket» is kept, since a dried herb is its own shelf); else Jev's pick of our categories (core.matching `CATEGORIES`, matprat's 35 shelves for now, since the rules go by them: the main protein, §3; how much a discount counts, §4; lactose only in the dairy categories, §1; the category search words, §6), each shown with the 12 names the vocabulary files there that the dinners use most, and no dinner as context. Then its facts, from Jev with that category as context. Probed on 200 of matprat's names it was not shown (2026-10-14), the pick put 158 in matprat's own department and 26 in one that changes a rule: mostly near misses (tranebær as /bær, medisterdeig as /kjøtt/pølser, avokado as /frukt), some a protein gained or lost (bbq-saus as pølser, smult as melk, uerfilet as storfe). 30 names per department, other wording, and the kind of meat asked apart did no better (27 to 34 such errors); a vote by word stems over matprat's names, as for offers, was worse (20 to 30 % of names). Nothing is asked twice: a wrong row is fixed by hand (above).
- Until 2026-10-22 the names only godt.no uses were asked on every build, labels and department alike, with the first dinner by URL they are in, and a pick of meat, fish, shellfish or dairy the labels did not agree with became /diverse with «maybe» for what it holds. The dinner helped rare words (and, elg, bog, breiflabb, kveitekotelett and tofu came right; 55 of 750 answers changed, some too far the safe way, gulbeter in a pork dinner as pork) and hurt common ones: erter's first dinner is «Omelett med skinke, løk og poteter», and the build of 2026-10-22 filed erter as /egg with egg «maybe». The same two requests to jev-1.14.0, eight times in a row, gave egg «yes» 0.36 to 0.60 and /egg 0.33 to 0.67; with no dinner, or with three other dinners erter is in, /egg was 0.00 to 0.01; jev-1.13.0 leaned the same way (/egg 0.23). gulost went to /egg from an omelette the same way, and the doubt check made «vegetar» leave out and (duck) although its labels said no meat. So the dinner, the doubt check and the re-asking are gone, and with them the labels' chance to change from one build to the next (3 to 5 % of dinners changed a diet verdict between the builds of 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-22).
- Offers are only looked up for ingredients that matter for the shop (§4 weights), via a word filter: the first 5 letters of each word of the ingredient name in the offer heading ("lakse" finds LAKSEFILET), skipping adjectives like grønn, rød, fersk (src/core/matching.py `_FILLER`).
- A heading with pålegg, chips, burger, nuggets, kjeks, sjokolade, godteri, postei, smårett or snacks is no ingredient, unless the ingredient's own name has the word (hamburgerbrød). A heading equal to the ingredient name was a match without asking until 2026-10-22; it is asked about now, since King Oskar's «Tunfisk» is a tin.
- Only discounted offers are looked up (jb 2026-10-22: "make sure that we do not waaste effort on dishes that do not have any discounts"): nothing else reaches a card, so a dinner without one costs no question. On 2026-10-22's offers that was 1 082 of 3 341.
- Meat, fish and shellfish are matched per dinner, with the form the recipe line asks for and the dinner's name in the question; other ingredients by product (core.matching `per_dinner`). Matched by name alone, Jev gave «Hjemmelaget nigiri sushi» King Oskar's tinned tuna in dressing (jb 2026-10-22: "im pretty sure that the cheep tuna, is canned, not really something you want to use with sussu?"), and the same flaw gave smoked salmon for laks, cured spekeskinke for skinkestek, grilled ribbe from the hot counter for ribbe, and elg for lam. The recipe's line seldom says the form: 417 of 3 661 meat and fish lines name one (røkt, lettsaltet, fersk, boks, …), and the nigiri's line is just «tunfisk», so the dinner is the signal, and the form goes with it as one word kept in dishes.json (core.dishes `FORMS`: the first the line names, so «1 boks tunfisk» is hermetisk; none means fresh or raw, as the name alone is meant) rather than the line itself, which dishes.json does not keep. A flag per form on the offer's side (canned, smoked, …) was tried and dropped as too narrow. On 2026-10-22's offers this is 2 753 requests and 15 216 questions, against 598 and 4 785 by name with every offer, and 12 381 and 98 988 per dinner for every ingredient and offer.
- A question is about a product, the vocabulary's `product` for a name (the name itself until someone sets «kyllingfilet» for «kyllingfilet i strimler»), so two names of one product are asked once and both get the answer; the matches are written per name, as the site reads them. A refresh of the offers alone (`--offers`, FACTS.md §4) asks the same questions as a full build, since the form is in dishes.json; until 2026-10-22 it had no recipe lines and lost the 417 forms.
- The cards of the build of 2026-10-23 (jb's run, the first on the vocabulary) put 37 more rows right by hand: every stock, bouillon, fond and sauce the vocabulary filed under meat, fish or shellfish (kjøttkraft, oksekraft, kyllingbuljong, hønsebuljongterning, fiskesaus, viltkraft, rekepasta, …) is /diverse now, since a cook buys them in the dry-goods aisle and nothing at the meat counter is them: as /kjøtt they were the main protein of 50 dinners without solid meat («Chili sin carne med quinoa» as kylling, a gresskar risotto as storfe) and were asked about per dinner, where Jev bought VILTKARBONADE as viltkraft for 6 dinners. The facts stay: a stock holds meat. Three of them were sauces the hint dinner had made meat of: østerssaus (/kjøtt/pølser, meat yes, pork maybe, from «Svinespyd med østerssaus og kokosmelk»), sweet chili saus and plommesaus. dishes.json's parts followed: 609 parts over 37 names.
- Jev's yes to an offer is wrong most often on a Norwegian product name it does not know, or a word inside another word. On 2026-10-23: potet got MAARUD POTETGULL (chips; 239 of the dinners shown), nudler COOP KOPPNUDLER, smøreost Smøremyk (a butter), fersken Fersk pasta, naturris Tine yoghurt naturell, carnaroli-ris Ristorante (a pizza), ekte geitost TERINA EKTE INNHERREDSODD (a tinned stew), grønne bønner ALI HELE BØNNER (coffee), salatost Potetsalat, baby leaf salat Salatost, kokt skinke Serranoskinke, skinkebiff SKINKE XXL, hamburger COOP HAMBURGERRYGG, røkt laks Lakserøre (a spread). potetgull and koppnudler joined core.matching `NOT_AN_INGREDIENT`, which needs no Jev; the rest wait for a probe of the question (TODO.md). The form does its job: the tinned tuna on offer is served to «Fiskekaker med tunfisk» («1 boks tunfisk») and the salads, not to «Tunfisk poke» or «Hjemmelaget nigiri sushi».

## 3. Main protein and variety

- A dinner's main protein is its heaviest meat, fish or shellfish ingredient by grams, grouped by matprat department (fisk, skalldyr, kylling, kalkun, and, kjøttdeig, svin, pølser, storfe, lam, vilt; other meat is "kjøtt"; none is "vegetar"). matprat's own main-ingredient label is not used, since it files "Kyllingfilet med spekeskinke" under pork. Stock and other liquids (dl, l, ss, …) only count when nothing solid is there: matprat files kjøttkraft under /kjøtt, and its stock made «Svinekoteletter med hjemmelaget surkål» a beef dinner. This changed the main ingredient of 122 of 3 399 dinners and the protein of 58 (2026-10-22). Since 2026-10-23 the vocabulary files stocks, bouillons and sauces under /diverse (§2), so none is a main protein: 50 dinners with no solid meat or fish («Chili sin carne med quinoa» with kyllingbuljong) became vegetar or took their next ingredient.
- The list puts the best 3 of each protein first and the rest after, unless a protein or a word is searched for. It used to stop at 3 per protein, which showed 39 of the 1 908 dinners at most. While the page showed a week above it (until 2026-10-14), the list left out the week's dinners when nothing was searched. Since 2026-10-22 the site, the API and the CLI leave out every dinner without an ingredient on sale near the place (jb: "We should ignore dinners with 0 Discounted ingredients"; app.queries `Queries.ranked`): 1 978 of 3 399 in Stavanger, 2 247 with every store. From the same day they also leave out every dinner under 20 kr off in all (core.ranking `FLOOR_KR`; jb: "no point in showing 100 menues with tuna on sale for 8kr"): with the offers of 2026-10-22, 2 262 of 3 392 dinners had any kroner off, 1 467 had 20 or more, 1 180 had 30 or more and 582 had 50 or more, and the median among those on sale was 31 kr. The floor is a guess to tune on the cards. On the cards of the build of 2026-10-23, after the hand fixes (§2), Stavanger lists 865 dinners: 371 save 20 to 30 kr and the main protein is what is on sale in 259 of them (kjøttdeig and kyllingfilet offers of 12 to 25 kr), 93 save 30 to 40 kr (70), 43 save 40 to 50 (34), 358 save 50 or more (338). A floor of 30 would drop mostly real meat deals, so 20 stays.
- Until 2026-10-22 a week was drawn too: one dinner per protein, except fish up to twice and never the same fish ingredient twice (jb 2026-10-13: "load of chicken options"), filled with repeats when too few proteins fit. jb: "We are removing the week's draw"; it is gone from the API, the CLI, profile.yaml and llms.txt.

## 4. Offers and discounts

- An offer is on sale when it has a before-price above today's price: Tjek's field, or "Førpris"/"Før" in the text, using the lower end of a range. Catalog items without one count for nothing, even though they are in the weekly catalog (jb 2026-10-13: "dinners we pick are based of discounts").
- When the price is a tenth of the per-kg price (priced per 100 g) and the before-price is per kg, the before-price is divided by 10. Discounts above 75 % are dropped as unit mix-ups.
- A dinner is on sale when its main protein is discounted; without meat or fish, a vegetable from 100 g per portion. Discounted bacon on the side does not make lutefisk a dinner on sale.
- Weight (core.matching.weight) says which ingredients are looked up among the offers at all, and which comes first on a card: 3 for meat, fish and shellfish, 1 for eggs, vegetables, potatoes and mushrooms, 0.5 for fruit, cheese and rice/pasta, scaled by amount up to 100 g per portion; liquids (dl, ss) count at most 0.5, since matprat files stock under meat (the vocabulary files it under /diverse since 2026-10-23, §2); 0 (spices, oil, flour) is not looked up. Until 2026-10-22 the ranking was weight times discount, summed; it is the kroner off now (§5). Moving from 400 g per recipe to 100 g per portion changed recipes for 2 or 6 portions: 639 dinners on sale instead of 626 (2026-10-13).
- No prices per dinner: jb asked for candidates that fit, not cost estimates (2026-10-13).
- Nor kroner a head or for the recipe's need. Both were built on 2026-10-23 (every amount in g, ml or stk on both sides, a piece weight per name from Jev as the bridge, the saving scaled to the recipe, «ca.» for estimates) and dropped the same day on jb's word ("I regret all of this cost calculations, lets trim down all of it, we are just going to try to correlate recepies with in sale ites from the ads, thats it. No extra cost calculations, it gets tricky"): the sum stays the catalogs' own saving per pack (§5), and an offer matched to an ingredient is the whole claim. The code, the plan with the research and the week's numbers are in the history, 51e8969 to 6034314.
- Places: 20 towns spread over the country, with the stores within 15 km (the radius Stavanger had). Between them they reach every regional catalog seen around them on 2026-10-13 (Extra's six, Bunnpris's four, KIWI and SPAR north and south). A visitor picks one in «Sted»; a place is not a search word (jb 2026-10-14), so «bergen» in the box looks for the word, as in «Bergensk fiskesuppe».
- Without a place the offers are those of every catalog in every place, not only those in all places (jb 2026-10-13: "We can assume all shops until the users narrows by selecting a location"): the catalogs in all 20 places listed 260 offers, against 580 to 1 015 in one place. So a card can show a regional price from far away until a place is picked, and «Sted» says «Hele landet».

## 5. Ranking

- The most kroner off first, every discounted ingredient summed (§6; until 2026-10-22 weight times discount, §4), then rating (rating − 4, from 5 ratings on) and prep time (under 20 min +1, 20–40 +0.5, 40–60 0, over 60 −1).
- Until 2026-10-22 a week was also drawn from the dinners on sale, anew on every call (/api/plan and `matris plan`; the site showed none since 2026-10-14; jb 2026-10-14: "the lowest prices, in rabatt kr, if we can make this semi random"): per main protein the 5 with the most kroner off, in a random order weighted by the square of the kroner off (Efraimidis and Spirakis), which kept 1 853 different weeks in 2 000 draws. Gone with the draw (§3).
- Because discounts come before rating, a three-star dish over an hour can top the list when its meat is heavily discounted (open question in TODO.md).
- "De fleste til" on the site is the median end date of the offers, since a few catalogs run for months.

## 6. Search (src/core/search.py)

jb 2026-10-13: "more categories like; uten løk, glutenfree etc… an epic search… highly fit to our need, but it needs to be human friendly".

- The diet rules are search words, each a chip in «Kosthold» or «Allergier» (core.search.Diet; the grouping is web.main's DIET_CHIPS, ALLERGY_CHIPS and STOMACH_CHIPS): «vegetar» (also vegetarisk, kjøttfri), «vegansk», «pescetarianer» (also «uten kjøtt»), «glutenfri», «melkefri» («uten melk»), «lite laktose» (3 g per portion), «uten løk» (vårløk is fine), «uten hvitløk», «lav fodmap», «lavkarbo» (keto), «uten svin», «uten alkohol», «gravid», and «uten X» for egg, fisk, skalldyr, bløtdyr, nøtter, peanøtter, soya, sesam, selleri, sennep, sulfitt, lupin and belgfrukter (also eggfri, nøttefri, …). They change which dinners fit, on the site from no rules at all (core.search.ANYTHING), in the CLI on top of profile.yaml. The week follows the rules picked. «uten fisk», «uten svin» and «uten kjøtt» used to leave out dinners by matprat's department; they are rules now and also catch fiskesaus, bacon and kyllingbuljong.
- The other categories narrow down the dinners that fit. «Ingen bytter», «uten hvitløk» and the diet categories «as written» are gone (jb 2026-10-13: "Unsure what the 'ingen bytter' part is"; "This clutter hell"); «uten hvitløk» now leaves out dinners with garlic like any «uten X».
- Prep time uses matprat's buckets: «rask», «kjapp», «enkel» and «hverdag» are under 20 min; «under N min» is the strictest bucket that keeps within N (under 30 → under 20, under 45 → under 40, under en time → up to 60); over 90 min is ignored.
- «Godt likt» is a rating of 4.5 or more from at least 20 ratings (508 of 1 908 dinners; 559 of 3 399 with godt.no, 2026-10-14). «Barnevennlig» has no ingredient Jev calls spicy and no chili, spicy or sterk in the name (1 368 dinners; 2 244 with godt.no). «På tilbud» is `on_sale` (§4).
- Protein words (kylling, fisk, svin, gris, elg, vegetarisk, …) pick the main protein (§3); several mean either. «kjøtt» is any meat, «sjømat» fish or shellfish, «annet kjøtt» the group of other meat. «uten svin» leaves out any pork ingredient, so torsk med bacon goes too; «uten kjøtt» leaves fish and vegetarian.
- A «Hovedråvare» chip adds its group to the main proteins chosen or takes it out, and «Alle» clears them (jb 2026-10-14: "We need to allow multi select on ; Hovedråvare"). A chip is on when its group is among them, so «kjøtt» lights every meat chip; taking one out writes the rest as groups («sjømat» without fisk is «skalldyr»). An off chip counts the dinners with that main protein, which is what a click adds, since a dinner has one.
- Other words must be in the dish name or an ingredient name: «laks» finds røkelaks and laksefilet (Norwegian compounds: a word of 5+ letters anywhere, 3–4 letters at the start, or at the end of a word at least 4 letters longer, so «ris» finds jasminris but not kapris), and «tacos» finds taco. Dish kinds (suppe, gryte, salat, taco, pizza, wok, …) look at the name only, so «salat» is not every burger with lettuce. «sopp», «ost», «nøtter», «egg», «bær» and «skalldyr» also find matprat's department of that name (sjampinjong is /sopp). «pasta» also finds spagetti, tagliatelle, penne and the like.
- «uten X», «ikke X», «ingen X» and «-X» leave X out, and «og»/«eller» carry it on («uten sopp og paprika»). «uten løk», «uten gluten», «ikke sterk» and «ingen bytter» are the categories, not words. «X eller Y» is either; other words all have to match.
- Words that say nothing (jeg, vil, ha, middag, til, med, …) are skipped. æ, ø and å may be typed as ae, o and a; a word typed with them is matched as written («kål» does not find kalkun).
- A word that matches nothing is swapped for the closest known word (difflib, at least 75 % alike) and the page says so: «kyllign» becomes kylling.
- Dinners with a searched word in the name come before those with it only among the ingredients, then the ranking (§5) decides.
- When nothing matches, the page offers to drop each part of the search, with how many dinners that would give, most first.
- There is no sort since 2026-10-22 (jb: "we can drop the percent, i dont think anyone really cares about percent"; "we can remove the entire; Sorter - kr"): the ranking (§5) goes by kroner off. That is every discounted ingredient summed (core.models.Dinner.saving; jb 2026-10-22: "we should look at total saving per dinner due to the Discounted ingredients"), as the catalogs have it: before-price minus price, per pack, not per portion (jb 2026-10-22: "I dont think we should get into per portion, its a rabbit hole"). Two ingredients matched to the same offer count it once. Taking turns between offers was turned down (jb 2026-10-22: "it sounds confusing"); the best 3 of each main protein first (§3) keep the top of the list mixed.

## 7. godt.no (src/pipeline/godt.py)

- A godt.no recipe is a dinner when it has the «Middag» tag (54 of 120 pages sampled on 2026-10-14). That takes in a few sides and sauces tagged both «Middag» and «Tilbehør», as matprat's «Middagsrett» does.
- The recipe sitemap is 404 since 2026-10-22 (`/sitemap-recipes.xml`, and `/sitemap-index.xml`, which robots.txt still names). The index is `https://www.godt.no/oppskrifter/middag`, 24 dinners a page (1 456 on 2026-10-22, against 1 455 dinners in the build of 2026-10-14). The whole catalog is 3 902 recipes and the build keeps only dinners, so the other pages are not read. `changes.updated` stands in for the old `lastmod`. A list that comes back short of page 1's total is an error, so a broken page does not quietly drop dinners. Later pages under-counted by 9 the same day (1 447 against page 1's 1 456); the last page held 16, which is 1 456 at 24 a page, so the walk follows page 1. `--known` still reads only the URLs already in dishes.json, and misses dinners added since.
- Its minutes (before, cooking and after, as its `totalTime`) go in matprat's buckets, each holding its upper end: 20 minutes is «20–40 min», not «rask», and 60 is «40–60 min». A pizza dough that rises overnight is over 60 minutes.
- «1 - 2 ss» counts as the middle, 1.5 ss. godt.no's units are put in matprat's words (fedd is båt, stykker stk, skive(r) skive; `UNITS`), so the grams per unit are matprat's (§1).
- Its rating is out of 5, as matprat's, so «godt likt» and the ranking read both alike.
- A dish on both sites is two dinners, one per source.
